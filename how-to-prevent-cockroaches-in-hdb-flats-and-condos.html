Prevention in HDB flats and condominiums means reducing places where cockroaches can hide and enter. A cockroach control service begins with an inspection of high-risk areas. The treatment method is then chosen by species, because American, German, brown-banded and Oriental cockroaches may need different approaches. Gel baiting can be placed in cabinet hinges, cracks and appliance gaps. Residual insecticide can be applied around skirting, entry points and drain perimeters. Insecticidal dust can be used in wall voids, and granular bait can be used for outdoor and drain areas. Hiding and entry points are treated, and a written report follows each visit.

For flats and condos, prevention also includes following the after-treatment instructions. Re-entry is safe 2 hours after treatment, and treated surfaces should not be mopped for 48 hours. Mild to moderate infestations usually need 1 to 2 visits, while severe ones need 2 to 3 visits over 3 to 4 weeks.
